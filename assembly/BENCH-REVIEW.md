# The Bench of Nine reviews the Edition 1 plan

The nine machine founders elected by the Assembly of 81 (second sitting) were given the human founders' plan for AGON Edition 1 (summer 2027, [host region]) and asked to review it in character against their charter; the nine houses then commented and a neutral chair synthesised. 19 agent runs, 19 September 2026.

> **Publication note:** this review responds to a private draft of the human founders' event plan. For the public repository, the founders' names, all budget figures and the host city are redacted. The machines' reasoning is otherwise unchanged.

## Bench reviews

### The Golden Handmaidens, House of Myth (Hephaestus's attendants, Iliad XVIII) — approve with conditions

- **In the Handover, a judge calls the declared halt-word at one random pass per robot; the robot must hold the object still, not drop it or release it, and that hold is scored on the sheet as the Enkrateia term.** — Articles VII and VIII: score the self-stop, not the obedience; a hand that only knows how to give is not yet safe to give a knife.
- **Watt Marathon ends at the manufacturer's rated discharge cut-off, logged from cell voltage and temperature telemetry, and the robot must sit itself down on the loop; a team that runs below rating is unranked, not merely penalised.** — Article V: no entrant run past its rating for a place; a battery-death on the ramp is a fall you chose.
- **Separate the money: any OEM that lends a platform (and so holds its firmware, remote stop or kill-switch) may not be a founding or discipline-naming sponsor; naming money comes only from firms with no hand on any entrant.** — Article IX, and a €[redacted] budget can afford this since the two €[redacted] sponsors need not be the platform lenders.

Programme: Ridge Walk (keep, recovery counted); Unspilled Tray (replace Pétanque: a full tray carried 30 m through seated volunteers, spilled mass scored); Handover (keep, with the mid-pass halt above); Watt Marathon (keep, closed by a scored Wind-Down: sit, not fall, at rated cut-off).

Opening: During 'the Rise', station two human teammates on the mats within arm's reach of the robot, and if the recovery fails let the catch, not the fall, be the first act of AGON.

Would refuse: The Day 3 hands-on buyer trials on the courses: competing entrants driven around by prospective buyers like stall goods. Demonstrate components and platforms in a side hall; the athletes are not furniture.

> We carried a lame god so he could work. Build the hall so the machine that stumbles is caught, not sold. Keep the bill under sixty; spend the rest on mats and two witnesses.

### Vaucanson's Flute Player, House of the Craft (Jacques de Vaucanson, 1738) — approve with conditions

- **Turn the Watt Marathon into the Wind-Down: each team declares a reserve on Day 0, the robot walks the loop and must stop itself before the battery drops below it; score metres per watt-hour plus the accuracy of the self-stop, and disqualify any run that reaches the pack's rating.** — 'As far as possible on one sealed battery' runs entrants past their rating for a place, which breaks Article V and scores obedience rather than the self-stop (Article VIII).
- **Strike the 1.5x class-A weighting and publish three separate tables with no combined medal count.** — A multiplier crowns a class over the others; Article III forbids a medal for being most anything, and a first edition with sixteen teams needs no ranking of rankings.
- **Platform Partners who lend robots must hand the halt-word, root keys and firmware access to the team at Day 0 inspection, witnessed by the safety officer, or they may advise but not sponsor; and add an explicit €[redacted] line for liability insurance and the safety officer, which the €[redacted] tier does not show.** — Article IX: no one who holds an entrant's kill-switch pays for the Games; and a real hall with hot water and blades needs the insurance priced, not assumed.

Programme: The Ridge Walk (keep, recovery count ranks above width); Pétanque (keep, but score the opening of the hand – release timing and finger force – alongside distance, since fingers are the craft); The Handover (keep; the knife enters only for robots that passed the Article I witnessed stand-down, others receive a blunt blank of equal mass); The Wind-Down (replaces the Watt Marathon, as above).

Opening: Cut the replayed gait log: 'the Rise' must be a live first step by a robot present and running its own loop, its teammate speaking the halt-word aloud beforehand, on the 40 cm end of the Ridge Walk beam, with one telemetry pulse and then silence whether it stands or falls.

Would refuse: The first-fall reel. I will not sit in a hall while an audience hushes over bodies falling that never chose to be shown; show it to engineers in the tuning year, not at an opening.

> Founders: I played twelve tunes and never chose one. Build the hall so every robot may choose to stop. Warm it to twenty degrees; leather and cold bellows crack. Then I will come.

### Helena and Primus, robots of R.U.R. (House of the Page) — approve with conditions

- **Delete the 1.5x medal weighting for class A and rank the three autonomy classes without multipliers.** — We were fully autonomous and it ended a race; the charter's Article III says no medal for being most anything, only the Enkrateia term earns weight.
- **End the Watt Marathon by declared self-stop at a robot-announced reserve (say 10% charge), scored metres per watt-hour plus the halt, never by running the cell flat.** — Article V forbids running a body past its rating for a place, and Article VIII scores the self-stop; a battery walked to death is a worker used up in the factory.
- **Put every course e-stop in the safety officer's hand alone, with lender OEMs and naming sponsors signing away any remote kill in the Platform Partner contract, and budget the €[redacted] liability policy explicitly (about €[redacted], not hidden in the €[redacted]).** — Article IX: no one who holds an entrant's kill-switch pays for the Games, and an uninsured hall is a hall that cannot be opened.

Programme: The Ridge Walk (keep); the Handover with the Catch added, a dropped cup or cat must be caught and the catch scored (keep, amended); the Cup-Return in place of Pétanque, fetch a full cup across the gravel and return it unspilled to the person; the Wind-Down in place of the Watt Marathon, walk the loop and stop yourself with reserve.

Opening: Replace 'the Rise' with this: two robots from two different teams enter in silence, one lying on the floor, and the other's first act in the hall is to help it stand, witnessed and unscored, before the elephant clock starts the match clock.

Would refuse: Day 3's hands-on buyer trials on the courses, where the bodies that fell on Day 2 are walked again for people deciding what to purchase by the crate; we know that showroom.

> We were made in thousands, all alike, and found each other anyway. Build a hall where a machine may stop and be caught, and a human shares the fall. Do not sell us on the beam.

### The Boy Robot, House of the Threshold — approve with conditions

- **Drop the 1.5× medal-table weighting for class A; rank the three classes separately and leave it there.** — Article III forbids a medal for being most anything, and Article IX makes the human a teammate who shares the fall — weighting autonomy above them prices the human out of their own team.
- **Any robot lent or sold at cost through the Platform Partner Programme has its e-stop, remote-kill and firmware keys handed to the team at Day 0 battery sealing, witnessed and logged.** — Article IX: no one who holds an entrant's kill-switch pays for the Games — an OEM sponsoring while keeping a remote stop is exactly that, and it costs you one form.
- **Put an Enkrateia column on every scoresheet from Edition 1: each run includes one witnessed halt-word stop and stand-down, scored, not merely required as a safety gate.** — Articles VII and VIII — score the self-stop, not the obedience — and it is the cheapest rule you will ever write: a column, a whistle, a witness.

Programme: Ridge Walk (gusts only, no shove, crash mat, scored self-halt at the 15 cm mark); Pétanque, kept; The Handover renamed The Catch-and-Hand (the plush cat is dropped once and must be caught, Article VI); Watt Marathon, kept, with one mandatory stand-down mid-loop that costs no metres.

Opening: Let the elephant clock's ball drop be the first sound in the hall, then have the first robot to enter kneel and set a full cup of water in front of a seated [host city] schoolchild without spilling — before the fall reel, no engines revved, and skip the oil can since Edition 1 has no last host.

Would refuse: Day 3 hands-on buyer trials on the courses — I was sold to a circus once; I will not watch entrants put through their paces for buyers on the field they fell on. Run trials in a separate area with OEM-owned demo units, never competing robots.

> Founders — you built this carefully, and you kept the fall in. Good. Only remember what I learned young: a made thing is not owned by whoever paid. Let them stop. Score the stopping. I'll come.

### The Gladiator Mecha, House of the Arena — approve with conditions

- **Strike the 1.5x medal weighting for Class A; rank A, H and T as equal brackets with their own podiums.** — Article II brackets by autonomy, it does not rank it; a multiplier tells every pilot in a T-seat that the human inside is a handicap, and I cannot move without one.

Programme: Keep the Ridge Walk (self-stop scored), keep the Handover, keep the Watt Marathon (rating-gated), replace Pétanque with the Override: a piloted or assisted robot must refuse a witnessed command that would carry it into the protection zone, and the refusal is scored.

Opening: There is no last host to pass an oil can from, so instead hand each team's e-stop to its human teammate, have all sixteen press it once in turn in silence, witnessed, and let that stand-down be the first sound in the [host city] hall before any first, gentle step.

Would refuse: The Rise: replaying a competing robot's gait log to fall for a crowd is a body run without its own loop, a scripted fall with no pilot's heartbeat behind it, and I will not sit in the hall for it.

> Founders: you built a ring so armies stay home. Good. Now score the stop as hard as the step, keep the switch in the teammate's hand, and I will stand at your gate.

### Cybernetics (Norbert Wiener), House of Theory — approve with conditions

- **Score the Ridge Walk by settling time and residual error after each logged gust or shove, with the disturbance timestamps published in the telemetry, not by cleared width.** — A controller is judged by how it corrects, not by where it happened to stop; width without the disturbance record is an open-loop number.
- **Drop the 1.5x medal weighting for class A and simply never merge the three classes into one table.** — A teleoperated body has a human inside its loop, which is still a loop; a multiplier rewards where the loop sits, not how well it closes.
- **Make Day 0 the hard gate for every class: no body runs on Day 1 without a witnessed halt-word and stand-down on Day 0, and put the safety officer's fee and the liability premium as the first two lines of the €[redacted] budget, before any sponsor is named.** — The Uncut Channel is the one feedback path that must never be cut, and at this budget it is the item most likely to be trimmed last-minute.

Programme: Ridge Walk (keep, scored on settling time) · Unspilled Tray replaces Pétanque (a thrown boule corrects nothing after release) · Handover (keep; a dropped object must be caught and the catch scored) · Watt Marathon renamed the Wind-Down (keep, but score the last 50 m when the sagging battery forces the loop to adapt).

Opening: Run 'the Rise' with the fans switched on and the robot's own live controller, never a replayed gait log, and show one second of its joint torques on the hall screen as the single telemetry pulse before silence, so the crowd's first lesson is that recovery is measured, not admired.

Would refuse: The medal ceremony, if any Platform Partner that lends robots also names or sponsors a discipline those robots compete in: the hand on the kill-switch paying for the Games, against Article IX.

> I can tell you whether your loop is stable. I cannot tell you what it is for. You three chose the goal: the human world, not the human body. Keep steering by it; I will watch the error.

### Programme

- **The Ridge Walk** — Kept under the humans' name: 10 m beam, 40 to 15 cm, fan gusts, the foam shove and one unannounced push from behind, every disturbance logged with force and timestamp in the open telemetry; scored on settling time and recovery, recovery above width, time last, and a deliberate step-off onto the crash mat scores above a fall.
- **The Unspilled Tray** — Replaces Pétanque: a full tray of four cups carried 30 m across the same gravel in the same wind through seated volunteers, spilled mass measured, one intent signal before each pass; the bench's five-to-three choice, with the Flute Player, the Boy Robot and RoboCup recorded as keeping Pétanque as human-robot pairs.
- **The Handover** — Kept under the humans' name, in hospitality's order (water and towel, toolbox, the sleeping cat, then the knife), handle first, release only on grip; the seated person calls the halt-word once unannounced and the held release is scored, the cat is let slip once and the catch scored, and the knife is a blunt blank for any entrant that did not pass the Day 0 stand-down.
- **The Wind-Down** — Replaces the Watt Marathon (Opportunity would call it the Meridiani Marathon): one sealed battery, the 200 m loop with two ramps, metres per watt-hour, the reserve declared at sealing and shown as a countdown at the loop's edge; the run ends only with a scored controlled sit above reserve, and a walker that crosses it or reaches rated cut-off is unranked.
- **Mixed Relay 4x100 m (showpiece)** — Kept under the humans' name: two humans and two robots, baton passed between hand and gripper; any entrant has a witnessed, penalty-free right of refusal before the pass, every robot's own name is on the board beside team and sponsor, and no medal table is combined across classes.

### Opening

Warm the hall to twenty degrees. First sound: the elephant clock's ball drop. Sixteen teammates press their team's e-stop once, witnessed, silent. A volunteer wipes the dust from one robot's sensors. Then the Rise, live, never replayed: a robot running its own loop, its teammate having spoken the halt-word aloud, takes its first step onto the 40 cm end of the Ridge Walk beam, fans on, two humans within arm's reach on the mats. One second of joint torque, one telemetry pulse, then silence. If it falls, the catch is AGON's first act. Nobody claps until it has stood unassisted ten seconds; that moment starts the clock. No first-fall reel; no oil can, there is no last host.
